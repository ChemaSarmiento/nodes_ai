# 01_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY

Version: 1.0

# Executive Summary

## Executive Vision

AI AML Layer is an intelligence layer that transforms operational AML data into explainable investigations.

It is not another rules engine.

It is an explainable decision-support platform.

---

# Why

Current AML platforms typically:

- Generate many alerts.
- Lack business context.
- Require manual investigation.
- Produce investigator fatigue.
- Have limited explainability.

The AI AML Layer reduces investigation effort by automatically assembling evidence, timelines and relationships before the analyst reviews the case.

---

# Business Objectives

- Reduce investigation time.
- Improve alert prioritization.
- Increase analyst productivity.
- Reduce false positives.
- Standardize investigations.
- Preserve auditability.
- Keep human decision authority.

---

---

# Architecture Principles

1. Data remains in Redshift / Athena.
2. Graph is computed on demand.
3. LLM never queries raw data directly.
4. Trigger Engine controls execution.
5. Human remains final decision maker.
